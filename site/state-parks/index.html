Who may use a Florida State Park dump station

In short. The fee schedule says $10 per unit for a non-registered camper. Blue Spring says $10.65. Manatee Springs says its dump station is closed. Other park pages quoted here state who may use the station.

This page quotes Florida State Parks pages that say who may use a dump station, what a person who is not a registered camper pays, or that a station is closed. It is not a list of every state park dump station. A page that only says a station exists is not given a row. The quoted rules on this page do not use OpenStreetMap or any other community listing.

Call ahead. Hours, fees, gate codes and who may use a station change often, and many park stations are for registered campers only. Confirm with the operator before you drive there.

What the fee schedule says

The Division of Recreation and Parks posts fee-schedule PDFs on floridastateparks.org. On 27 October 2026, two files whose names include 2021_05_10 were read. One of those file names also includes 05.2022. Both contain this line: "Dump Station Per unit, non-registered campers $10.00". The two files are not the same in every section. The difference found is about an adoptive-family entrance pass, not the dump station. A third posted file, whose name includes 2013_10_01, contains the same dump-station line. [source, source, source]

The schedule says fees are per day unless otherwise noted. The dump line says "Per unit". It does not define a unit beyond those words. The dump line is not marked with the double asterisk the schedule uses for "Includes all state and local taxes."

The schedule also says: "Please confirm the recreational activity in which you want to participate is available at the location and on the date you plan to visit. Prices and availability subject to change without notice. County surcharges may apply." It gives the statewide information line as (850) 245-2157. It does not say that every park has a dump station. It does not say a registered camper pays nothing. It does not say whether a park entrance fee is charged in addition to the $10.

The schedule says this authority comes from Chapter 258, Florida Statutes, and Rule 62D-2.014, Florida Administrative Code. Section 258.014(1)(a) says the Division of Recreation and Parks "shall have the power to charge reasonable fees, rentals, or charges for the use or operation of facilities and concessions in state parks." That section does not name a dump station and does not set a dollar amount. [source]

Where official pages do not match

Blue Spring State Park says: "The fee is $10.65 to use the dump station if you are not a registered camper." The fee schedule says $10.00 per unit for non-registered campers. Both are Florida State Parks sources. They do not match. Neither page says that $10.65 is $10 plus tax, so this page does not treat it that way. [source, source]

The Manatee Springs State Park home page says: "Effective Jan. 14, 2026: The Waterfront Restroom and dump station at Manatee Springs State Park are currently closed." The experiences and amenities page says: "There is no sewer hook-up in Magnolia 2 or Hickory but a dump station is provided." The home page does not say that another dump station remains open. The amenities page does not say the station is closed. [source, source]

What these pages do not say

  • No hours page in the table states a separate opening time for the dump station.
  • The fee schedule does not say registered campers pay nothing. "Free" is included above only where a park page says free.
  • The camping page says: "Full-facility campsites for tent campers and RVers include water, electricity, a grill and picnic table, along with centralized showers, restrooms and dump station." It does not name the parks. It does not state a fee, and it does not say a day visitor may use the station. It gives the reservation phone as 800-326-3521 or TDD 888-433-0287. [source]
  • The Florida State Parks FAQ says: "Most parks have a central dump station." It does not name the parks, and it does not say who may use one. [source]
  • A park page that only says a dump station exists, and does not say who may use it, is not in the table. Stations this guide already quoted from an operator page stay on the regional lists.
  • The quoted rules on this page do not come from OpenStreetMap or another community listing. The map uses OpenStreetMap tiles. Each pin is a street address from that park's own page, matched by OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
