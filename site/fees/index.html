Florida RV dump station fees and access

Most sources do not publish a dump fee. Below are the only fee statements we found, then a summary of access rules.

Call ahead. Hours, fees, gate codes and who may use a station change often, and many park stations are for registered campers only. Confirm with the operator before you drive there.

Access summary

  • Florida State Parks dump stations are described as campground amenities. Anastasia says free for park campers [source]. Day-visitor use is not stated on the other park pages we read. Rainbow Springs says a day-use reservation program began on April 29, 2026, and that all visitors need a reservation to enter the park. That page does not say a day visitor may use the dump station [source]. State park camping reservations: 800-326-3521 [source].
  • Love's lists RV Dump Service among amenities at the four Florida locations we checked; price not stated on the location page [source].
  • FDOT rest areas: no dump stations stated [source].
  • OpenStreetMap access tags: 14 customers only, 2 public, rest not stated [source].
